Our Purpose

We believe that when women come together with humility, compassion and determination, they can create lasting positive change in the lives of individuals, families and communities.

The HDS Women Poverty Alleviation Program channels that belief into direct action — supporting women with the resources, skills and encouragement they need to build more stable, dignified lives.
